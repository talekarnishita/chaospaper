LLE and Chaos Results — Validation Report
============================================================

1. LOGISTIC MAP (known chaotic, LLE_theory = ln(2) ~ 0.693)
--------------------------------------------------
   Theoretical LLE (from f'(x)): 0.6930
   Rosenstein LLE (our implementation): 0.1280
   VALID: Both positive; Rosenstein correctly flags chaos.
   Note: Rosenstein on scalar embedding often underestimates vs direct formula.

2. WHITE NOISE (stochastic; expected LLE ~ 0 or negative)
--------------------------------------------------
   LLE over 5 realizations: mean = 0.0411, std = 0.0008
   CAVEAT: Mean LLE small positive (0.0411). Rosenstein on finite-length
   noise can yield small positive LLE; stochastic vs chaos should use
   0-1 test + surrogates as primary.

   IMPORTANT: The white noise LLE (~0.04) is of the same order as the
   NHL team LLE values (~0.04). This means the Rosenstein LLE alone
   CANNOT distinguish between NHL team series and white noise. LLE > 0
   is necessary but NOT sufficient evidence for chaos. The primary
   classifier is the 0-1 test + surrogate framework; LLE provides
   supplementary corroborating evidence only.

3. NHL TEAM 52 — Consistency (LLE vs 0-1 test)
--------------------------------------------------
   Team 52 FTHG: N = 888, LLE = 0.0427
   0-1 test (chaos_modified / run_chaos_octave.m): Result = chaotic.
   CONSISTENT: LLE > 0 and 0-1 test = chaotic; both point toward chaos.
   However, given the white noise caveat above, LLE alone would not be
   sufficient — the classification rests primarily on the 0-1 test.

4. SUMMARY VERDICT
--------------------------------------------------
   * Logistic map (chaotic): Rosenstein LLE > 0 — PASS.
   * NHL Team 52: LLE > 0 and 0-1 test chaotic — PASS (consistent).
   * White noise: LLE ~0.04 (small positive); demonstrates that LLE > 0
     alone is NOT sufficient to confirm chaos on finite samples.

   Conclusion: The 0-1 test + surrogate framework is the primary classifier
   for chaos vs stochastic. LLE provides supporting evidence: a positive
   exponent is consistent with the chaotic classification from the 0-1 test.
   However, because the Rosenstein estimator yields comparable small positive
   LLE for both NHL teams and white noise (~0.04), LLE should not be cited
   as independent confirmation of chaos. When presenting LLE to reviewers,
   frame it as "consistent with" the 0-1 test result, not "confirming" chaos.
